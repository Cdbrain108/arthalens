Meet the research team

Financial

Sales, profit, margins, debt, cash.

Valuation

Is the price cheap or expensive for what you get?

Market

Price moves, volume, 52-week range.

News

Why you can trust it

  • Every number has a source. Click a number to see where it came from.
  • Maths is done by code, not guessed by AI.
  • Gaps are shown, not hidden. If something could not be confirmed, the report says so.
  • Both sides are argued. Bull case and Bear case sit side by side.
  • Learning tool, not advice. Decisions are yours.

FAQ

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a research summary for learning. It does not know your money, taxes or plans.

Where does the data come from?

Market and company data from free public sources, news headlines from public feeds, and documents you upload. Each item lists its source and date.

Why does it sometimes say "could not confirm"?

Because inventing a number would be worse. Honest gaps make the report more trustworthy.
