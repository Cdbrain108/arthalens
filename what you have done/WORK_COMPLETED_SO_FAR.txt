1. AUDIT AND EVIDENCE-FIRST MERGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Completed a read-only audit of the handoff implementation, frontend, backend,
tools, LangGraph workflow, storage, RAG, PDF generation and tests.

Key findings recorded in:
- artha-ai/docs/HANDOFF_AUDIT.md
- artha-ai/CHANGELOG_HANDOFF_MERGE.md
- artha-ai/docs/ARCHITECTURE_FRAMEWORK.md

The application now follows these rules:
- LLMs explain; Python computes.
- Every numeric claim retains inputs, formula, period and basis where available.
- Missing evidence remains unavailable instead of becoming a guess.
- Conflicts are disclosed and cannot silently support an investment classification.
- Sources retain publication dates separately from retrieval timestamps.
- A successful job does not imply complete specialist coverage or independent
  verification of every source.
- The product is a research aid and not financial advice.

Report assembly no longer creates fabricated HOLD/BUY/SELL defaults, invented
benchmarks, canned company facts, unsupported quality scores or report-time
provider calls. Older saved reports may contain historical content; new research
must be run to generate reports under the current contracts.

5. CANONICAL AGENT REGISTRY AND LANGGRAPH EXECUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The backend now uses one executable specialist registry with eleven entries:
- financial
- valuation
- market
- news
- growth
- risk
- workforce
- industry
- management
- social
- document

Broad company research selects the nine core research domains. Document and
social coverage are added when relevant. Focused news questions route to news
only. Concurrency limits execution slots but never remove required agents from
the routed scope.

The HTTP API now invokes the compiled LangGraph directly. The graph stages are:

analyze_query → create_plan → apply_routing_policy → run_specialists →
collect_results → validate_evidence → challenge_thesis → synthesize_report →
build_report → generate_pdf

Specialist execution now has:
- Shared bounded collection concurrency.
- Independent timeout and failure handling.
- Partial, unavailable, failed and timed-out statuses.
- Persisted per-agent status and evidence counts for UI polling.
- Stable result ordering based on routed order.
- Source-owned evidence that a model cannot replace.

The new shared collectors replace canned deterministic findings. They invoke
the configured tools for financial statements, quarters, cash flow, valuation,
market history, technicals, volume, benchmark context, web evidence, official
ATS listings and selected documents.

Important safeguards:
- Financial ratios and growth are computed by Python tools.
- Calculation inputs and formulas are retained.
- Zero values are valid values and are not treated as missing.
- Workforce results do not invent hiring or attrition metrics.
- LinkedIn and forbidden social scraping domains are rejected.
- Selected document IDs are checked at retrieval and collection boundaries.
- Failed Indian listings cannot silently fall back to a different exchange.
- Point-in-time EPS is not presented as historical P/E evidence.

Registry and health endpoints expose local configuration status. Provider
connectivity is explicitly reported as not checked by the health endpoint.
